# Tierras de Tinta · Arte v3

Creado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Assets finales en `games/tierras_de_tinta/assets/`:

- `heroes-cartoon-v3.webp`: atlas transparente 4×2, los siete héroes de source30 en el orden original y una mascota de tinta en la celda restante.
- `enemies-cartoon-v3.webp`: atlas transparente 4×2, ocho familias de monstruos; los jefes conservan escala y auras regionales.
- `environment-cartoon-v3.webp`: atlas transparente 4×4, árboles, rocas, flores, cristales, cofres, recursos, ruina, estanque, farol, hierba, poción y trampa de hielo.
- `refuge-cartoon-v3.webp`: imagen del título, refugio y banner canónico del alumno.

Prompts de producción:

1. **Héroes** — Production game sprite atlas for Tierras de Tinta. Exactly 8 individual full body fantasy heroes in equal 4 columns by 2 rows, each centered with feet near same baseline and transparent gutters, no overlap. Charming stylized 3D animated family film aesthetic, exaggerated heads/hands, expressive eyebrows, soft sculpted shapes, vibrant toy materials, readable silhouette, slight three quarter frontal view. Row 1: young blue swordsman, crimson female arcane duelist, stout golden shield knight with hammer, green female archer. Row 2: purple masked sorcerer with staff, navy older veteran duelist, white/cyan female oracle with orb, friendly ink sprite mascot. Transparent background, no ground, no text/frame/watermark; wide 2:1 atlas, consistent scale.
2. **Monstruos** — Production monster atlas, exactly 8 full-body creatures, equal 4×2 grid with consistent baseline and transparent gutters. Playful stylized 3D family movie aesthetic, round forms, expressive eyes, mischievous faces, oversized heads, readable colorful toy materials. Row 1: turquoise horned ink slime, purple floating wisp, emerald armored beetle, orange goblin brute with wooden club. Row 2: navy spectral ghost with cyan face, crimson hooded shaman, gold stone golem sentinel, violet owl oracle. No ground/labels/frame/watermark; all subjects inside their cells.
3. **Refugio** — Official game title backdrop and launcher banner, wide landscape 16:9. Welcoming fantastical forest refuge in a stylized 3D family animated film aesthetic, rounded colorful trees, whimsical book-shaped architecture, ink-blue stream, moss rocks, flowers, lanterns, golden evening light. Large round tree on right, cozy workshop in right-middle, open center path to a glowing library tower, quiet left third for title. Vivid teal foliage, friendly adventure, no characters/text/UI/logo.
4. **Entorno** — Top-down game prop atlas, 16 isolated props in equal 4×4 grid, orthographic three-quarter camera, rounded playful fairy tale forms and transparent gutters. Row 1: green tree, twisted bare tree, mossy boulder, flowerbed. Row 2: blue crystals, purple crystals, closed gold chest, open chest. Row 3: logs, purple ink bottle, ruined arch, violet pond. Row 4: camp lantern, grass/daisies, red health potion with heart motif, blue crystal slowing trap. No text, borders, overlap or ground background.

Se han conservado los originales y sus rutas. La conversión a WebP mantiene el canal alfa de los atlas. No se han cambiado identidades, armas, familias, preguntas ni fórmulas de combate por motivos de arte.

El banner se publica también como `assets/games/tierras-de-tinta-banner-v3.webp` en `lenguarcade-assets`, para compatibilidad con el panel de alumno desplegado antes de esta revisión.
